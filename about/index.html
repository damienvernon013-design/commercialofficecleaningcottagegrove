About Cottage Grove Office Cleaning

Cottage Grove Office Cleaning provides scheduled commercial and office cleaning to businesses in Washington, Dakota, and Ramsey Counties. We have been operating in the south metro for 22 years. Our service is available to commercial accounts only — professional offices, medical and dental suites, light industrial facilities, and property managers overseeing multi-tenant buildings.

We work exclusively in commercial environments, which means our crews are trained for what commercial facilities actually require: keypad entry and after-hours security protocols, floor care equipment sized for commercial hard floors and carpet tile, and the documentation practices that facility managers need for their own compliance records.

How We Approach Each Account

Every engagement starts with a walkthrough. We look at square footage, traffic patterns, floor types, restroom count, breakroom setup, and any areas with specific cleaning requirements. From that walkthrough we produce a written scope of work — signed off by you before the first crew arrives. That document is the standard we're held to, and it doesn't change without your approval.

Crew consistency matters in commercial cleaning. The same people are assigned to your account each visit. They learn your layout, your schedule, and what normal looks like in your space. If a crew member is unavailable, we notify you before your scheduled cleaning — not after the fact.

Service Area

Our primary service area covers Cottage Grove and the communities within 25 miles of it: Woodbury, Saint Paul Park, Newport, Hastings, Afton, Denmark Township, Grey Cloud Island, and Inver Grove Heights. We operate Monday through Friday. Weekend and holiday scheduling is available by prior arrangement.

Credentials

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations and maintain a commercial bond. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote. For specific coverage figures and current documentation, contact us during the quote process.
