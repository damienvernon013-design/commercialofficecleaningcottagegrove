Commercial Office Cleaning Service Areas Around Cottage Grove

Cottage Grove Office Cleaning serves the south metro Twin Cities area within a 25-mile radius of Cottage Grove. Our crews operate Monday through Friday and are based in Washington County. Every town listed below is within our normal operating radius, and we staff accordingly so we are not routing crews from the opposite side of the metro.

Woodbury Saint Paul Park Newport Hastings Afton Denmark Township Grey Cloud Island Inver Grove Heights

About Our Service Radius

We do not advertise metro-wide service we cannot staff consistently. Our 25-mile radius from Cottage Grove covers communities in Washington, Dakota, and the eastern edge of Ramsey County. Drive times from Cottage Grove to the furthest communities in our service area run 20–35 minutes under normal conditions.

If your facility is outside this radius, contact us. In some cases we can accommodate a location if it is near a community we already serve, or we can refer you to a vetted contractor.

Services Available in All Areas

Every town in our service area can access our full range of commercial office cleaning services: nightly office cleaning, conference room and breakroom cleaning, and office carpet and floor care.
