Nightly Office Cleaning in Grey Cloud Island, MN

A 1,600 sq ft administrative office in Grey Cloud Island Township, MN — standalone building adjacent to a materials storage facility on the Mississippi River floodplain. Reception, two private offices, server room (excluded), small break area, single-stall restroom. Three-nights-per-week schedule. High particulate load from the adjacent operation: nightly entry mat management, damp mop of LVT throughout on every visit, restroom and break area sanitation. Spring high-water season creates additional moisture intrusion at the exterior entry — noted as a crew flag item in the scope.

About This Service

Every nightly office cleaning engagement in Grey Cloud Island starts with a walkthrough of your specific facility. We do not substitute tasks without your approval, and we do not adjust quality based on how long a visit takes.
