Commercial Office Cleaning in Newport, MN

Newport is located approximately 5 miles northwest of Cottage Grove at the intersection of US-61 and I-494. The I-494 interchange makes Newport accessible from across the south metro, and the commercial properties along Hastings Avenue and Maxwell Avenue serve both local and regional businesses.

Newport Commercial Context

Three characteristics shape how we scope office cleaning for Newport facilities:

  • I-494/US-61 interchange activity: Newport's position at this interchange means several distribution, logistics, and light industrial businesses maintain office components on-site. These facilities often require cleaning crews to work around shift schedules and loading dock access, which we account for in the scope.
  • Hastings Avenue commercial corridor: A mix of professional offices, auto-adjacent service businesses, and small medical suites. Parking lots here accumulate significant sand and salt from the I-494 ramp salt trucks, which tracks into building entries through the full winter season.
  • Dakota County adjacency: Newport straddles the Washington/Dakota County line. Several addresses have Dakota County mailing addresses while physically sitting in Washington County. We serve both sides of this line without a surcharge.
