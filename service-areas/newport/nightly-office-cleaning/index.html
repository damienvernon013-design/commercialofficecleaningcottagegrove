Nightly Office Cleaning in Newport, MN

A 4,800 sq ft logistics company administrative office in Newport, MN off Hastings Avenue near I-494. Open-plan operations floor, two private offices, training room, two restrooms, staff kitchen. Nightly scope after 5:30pm shift change: operations floor desk surfaces and vacuum, manager office wipe-down, training room chair reset and whiteboard cleaning, restroom full sanitation, kitchen counters and sink, trash from 16 stations. Entry and breakroom floors damp-mopped nightly due to proximity to the warehouse loading area.

About This Service

Every nightly office cleaning engagement in Newport starts with a walkthrough of your specific facility. The written scope that results from that walkthrough is the binding standard for every visit. We do not substitute tasks without your approval, and we do not adjust quality based on how long a visit takes.

For Newport accounts, our crews operate out of the Cottage Grove base approximately 8–15 minutes away under normal conditions. Crew assignments are fixed, and the same crew returns for each scheduled visit.
