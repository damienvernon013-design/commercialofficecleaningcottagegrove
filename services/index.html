Our Commercial Office Cleaning Services in Cottage Grove

Cottage Grove Office Cleaning provides three core services to commercial accounts in Washington County and the surrounding south metro. Each service is scoped in writing before the first visit, priced per visit at a fixed rate, and staffed by an assigned crew.

Nightly Office Cleaning

Scheduled 5 nights per week. Covers workstations, common areas, restrooms, trash removal, and kitchen wipe-down. The foundational service for offices with daily foot traffic.

Conference Room & Breakroom Cleaning

High-contact surface sanitation, table and chair cleaning, appliance exteriors, sink and coffee station. Timed around your meeting schedule and kitchen use patterns.

Office Carpet & Floor Care

Recurring vacuuming and hard-floor maintenance, plus periodic deep cleaning. Minnesota winter salt and sand management is included in seasonal scopes for Cottage Grove accounts.

How Services Are Scoped

We don't offer off-the-shelf packages. Every account starts with a walkthrough, and the scope document comes from what we see in your specific space. Two offices of the same square footage can have very different cleaning requirements depending on floor types, restroom configuration, breakroom use, and traffic patterns.

If you need services we don't provide — window washing, carpet extraction as a standalone service, or hazardous material handling — we'll tell you that upfront rather than attempting it outside our scope and charging for the attempt.
