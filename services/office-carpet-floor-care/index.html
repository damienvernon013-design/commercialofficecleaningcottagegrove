Office Carpet & Floor Care in Cottage Grove, MN

Floor care is the element of commercial cleaning most likely to fail without a specific scope. Vacuuming carpets nightly is straightforward. Hard-floor maintenance requires the right sequence of tasks at the right intervals, and in Minnesota it also requires accounting for a six-month season of road salt and sand.

Carpet Care

Our nightly carpet scope covers vacuuming of all traffic areas with a commercial-grade vacuum, spot treatment of visible stains during the visit, and entry mat management. For periodic deep cleaning (hot water extraction), we scope and price that separately as it requires different equipment and scheduling. Hot water extraction in a standard commercial office is typically needed twice per year — we recommend once before winter and once after, when the salt and sand season ends.

Hard-Floor Maintenance

Hard floors in commercial offices — tile, luxury vinyl tile (LVT), laminate, and polished concrete — require a different task sequence than carpet. Daily sweeping and damp mopping keeps them presentable. Periodic deep scrubbing and finish maintenance keeps them protected. We scope both the daily and periodic components separately so you know what you're getting at each visit versus what happens quarterly.

Minnesota Winter Salt Management

This is the specific issue that separates Cottage Grove floor care from what a generic contractor scope covers. From roughly October through April, the intersection of MN-61 and 80th Street produces significant road salt and sand tracked into every building with a public entry. Without active entry management, that material scratches LVT and tile, creates a white haze on hard floors, and works into carpet near entries.

Our winter floor care scope for Cottage Grove accounts includes: entry mat assessment and rotation recommendations, damp mopping of entry zones on every nightly visit during winter, and a periodic scrub specifically targeting salt residue. These are not add-ons — they are part of the standard scope for any Washington County account with a hard-floor entry.

Floor Types We Work With

  • Commercial carpet tile and broadloom
  • Luxury vinyl tile (LVT) and luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile
  • Polished or sealed concrete
  • Laminate

We do not strip and refinish hardwood or apply specialty coatings that require licensed floor care technicians. If your space has wood flooring in a high-traffic office setting, we can discuss appropriate maintenance scope during the walkthrough.
