Nightly Office Cleaning in Cottage Grove, MN

Nightly office cleaning is the primary service for businesses with consistent daily foot traffic. It runs Monday through Friday, typically after your close of business, and covers every area that sees daily use.

What Nightly Office Cleaning Includes

The specific tasks are defined in your written scope, but a standard nightly scope for a Cottage Grove commercial office typically covers:

  • Workstation surface cleaning (desks, monitors, keyboards wiped or left per client preference)
  • Common area floors: vacuuming carpet, sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Restroom sanitation: toilets, sinks, mirrors, fixtures, floors, trash, restocking paper and soap
  • Trash removal from all areas, liner replacement
  • Kitchen and breakroom: counters, sink, exterior of appliances, table wipe-down
  • Lobby and entry zone: floor maintenance including salt and sand management in winter months
  • Conference rooms: surface wipe-down, chair reset, trash

Frequency Options

Nightly cleaning is 5 nights per week by default. We also offer 3-nights-per-week schedules for offices with lighter daily use. The scope document specifies exactly which tasks occur nightly versus which are weekly or monthly items within the recurring contract.

Who This Service Suits

Nightly cleaning is the right fit for offices with 10 or more regular occupants, facilities with daily client or patient traffic, buildings where restroom sanitation cannot wait until a weekly visit, and any space where floor appearance is a client-facing issue. Medical and dental offices, professional service firms, and property management offices in the Cottage Grove corridor are typical accounts.

Washington County Winter Considerations

From October through April, road salt and sand from MN-61 and County Road 80 gets tracked into every office entry in Cottage Grove. Nightly scopes include entry mat management and damp mopping of entry zones during winter months as a standard item.
