Commercial Office Cleaning Services Pricing in Cottage Grove, MN

We do not publish standardized rates because commercial office cleaning prices depend on variables that differ meaningfully between facilities: square footage, floor type mix, restroom count, cleaning frequency, after-hours access requirements, and whether specialized services like carpet extraction or hard-floor stripping are included.

What we can tell you is what drives the number up and what drives it down.

What Affects Your Quote

How Pricing Is Structured

We quote a fixed per-visit price. That price does not change between invoices unless the scope changes. We do not bill "time and materials" for standard recurring visits — you know what the invoice will be before it arrives.

One-time deep cleans and periodic services (carpet extraction, hard-floor stripping and refinishing) are quoted separately and are not included in the recurring per-visit rate unless specifically written into the scope.

Minnesota Winter Floor Care

Offices in Washington County deal with a 5–6 month season of road salt and sand tracked onto lobby and corridor floors. If your space has a hard-floor entry, your per-visit scope should include entry mat management, damp mopping of the entry zone, and periodic deep scrubbing to prevent salt haze buildup on tile or LVT. We build this into winter scopes for Cottage Grove accounts as a standard item, not an add-on.
