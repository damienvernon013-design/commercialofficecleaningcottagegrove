Why Cottage Grove Buildings Choose Us

There is no shortage of cleaning contractors in the south metro. Most will give you a competitive quote. The question is what happens six months in when the crew changes, the scope gets informal, and the invoice doesn't match what was agreed to. Our answer to that is written documentation and assigned crews — not a promise that "we're different."

22 Years in This Business

We have been operating in the south metro since 2003. That means we have seen what causes cleaning contracts to fail: inadequate scopes, high crew turnover, no documentation, and poor communication when something goes wrong. Our procedures exist specifically to address those failure points.

Written Scope on Every Account

The scope document we produce after the walkthrough is the legal and operational baseline for your account. It specifies what gets cleaned, how often, and by what method. If we miss something in the scope, the resolution is clear. If you want to add something, we amend the scope in writing and adjust the invoice accordingly. There are no verbal side agreements.

Consistent, Assigned Crews

Your account is assigned to a specific crew lead and crew. The same people return for every scheduled visit. They learn the quirks of your space: the floor drain that needs extra attention in winter, the conference room that runs heavy on Tuesdays, the keypad sequence for the back entrance. That institutional knowledge degrades every time the crew changes.

Insured and Bonded for 22 Years

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial operations and maintain a commercial bond. A certificate of insurance is provided with your written quote. We have maintained continuous coverage for 22 years, which is verifiable by any facility manager or property manager who requires documentation.

Honest Pricing

We quote a fixed per-visit price. That number doesn't change unless the scope changes. We don't low-ball the initial quote and let it creep. We also tell you when a scope request will change the price before adding it, not after.
