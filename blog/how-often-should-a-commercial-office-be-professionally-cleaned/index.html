How Often Should a Commercial Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

This is one of the most common questions from business owners shopping for commercial cleaning. The answer depends on several factors — and getting it right means you're not overpaying for services you don't need or undercleaning until things deteriorate. For a deeper breakdown by occupant count, see our full office cleaning frequency guide.

The General Rule of Thumb

For most office environments, two to three cleaning visits per week strikes the right balance between hygiene, cost, and necessity. High-traffic offices with more than 20 employees or client-facing spaces typically need nightly service.

Factors That Determine Frequency

Number of employees: more people means more mess, more high-touch surfaces, more restroom usage. Client traffic: if clients or customers visit your space regularly, cleanliness is tied to your brand — daily service is usually worth it. Layout: open-plan offices with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices with fewer shared touchpoints. Season: cold and flu season, and Minnesota's winter salt season, may warrant temporarily increasing frequency.

Frequency by Office Type

Small professional office (under 10 people): one to two times per week. Mid-size office (10–30 people): two to three times per week. Large open-plan office (30+): nightly. Medical or dental office: nightly, plus deep sanitization protocols.

Don't Forget Periodic Deep Cleans

Regardless of your regular cleaning frequency, schedule periodic deep cleans for carpets, floor waxing, and hard-to-reach areas. We offer these as add-ons for existing Cottage Grove-area accounts.
