Commercial Cleaning Standards for Medical & Dental Offices

Cleaning a medical or dental office is a fundamentally different task from cleaning a standard commercial office. The stakes are higher, the standards are stricter, and the cleaning protocols must reflect the sensitivity of the environment.

Why Standard Commercial Cleaning Isn't Enough

General commercial cleaners are trained for appearance and basic hygiene. Medical environments require a higher level of disinfection discipline — not just cleaning. The difference lies in the products used, the dwell times required, and staff awareness of infection-control basics.

What Medical Cleaning Includes That Standard Cleaning Doesn't

EPA-registered disinfectants with appropriate dwell times. Clear distinction between clean and dirty zones within the facility. Frequent disinfection of all patient-contact surfaces between appointments. Use of color-coded microfiber cloths to prevent cross-contamination between zones. Awareness of biohazard and sharps-adjacent areas — working around them safely without handling them.

Questions to Ask a Medical Cleaning Provider

  • Which EPA-registered disinfectants do you use?
  • Do you have experience cleaning medical or dental facilities specifically?
  • Can you document your cleaning protocols for our records?
  • Are your staff background-checked?
  • What is your process if a patient-contact surface is missed between appointments?
