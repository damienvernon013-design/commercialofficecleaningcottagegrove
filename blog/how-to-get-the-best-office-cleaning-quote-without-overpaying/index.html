How to Get the Best Office Cleaning Quote Without Overpaying

Getting a commercial cleaning quote is easy. Getting a good one takes a bit of preparation. Businesses that consistently pay fair prices for good service share a few common habits — and they all happen before the cleaning company walks through the door.

Know Your Square Footage

This sounds obvious, but many businesses give rough estimates. Cleaners price by the square foot — the more accurate your number, the more accurate your quote. Walk the space, measure common areas, and know which spaces are included versus excluded (storage rooms, server rooms, etc.).

Define Your Scope Before the Walkthrough

Before inviting providers in for a quote, write down exactly what you need: which rooms, which tasks, how often, and what the non-negotiables are. See our guide on how to write an office cleaning scope for a step-by-step process. This prevents providers from quoting a stripped-down service and prevents you from accepting something that won't actually keep your space clean.

Get Multiple Quotes

One quote gives you a number. Several quotes give you a market. The spread between the lowest and highest bids for identical work can be substantial. Middle-range quotes are usually the sweet spot — the cheapest bids often involve underqualified staff or hidden fees, while the most expensive aren't necessarily better.

Ask What's Not Included

Always ask providers to tell you what falls outside their standard quote. This surfaces hidden costs before you sign anything, not after.

Negotiate Strategically

Offer a longer contract in exchange for a locked-in rate. Ask if paying quarterly or annually reduces the monthly cost. Bundle services for a package discount where it makes sense. Compare quotes within the same window — quotes are typically valid for a limited period.
