Green Commercial Cleaning: Why Eco-Friendly Products Are Better for Your Business

The commercial cleaning industry uses an enormous volume of chemical cleaning products. Many contain compounds that contribute to indoor air pollution and can trigger allergies and asthma. Green commercial cleaning is the growing alternative — and increasingly, it's not just the environmentally responsible choice. It's a practical business choice too.

What Makes a Cleaning Product “Green”

Products evaluated for human health and environmental safety, low-VOC or fragrance-free formulations, and biodegradable formulations that break down naturally without leaving harmful residues.

Health Benefits for Your Employees

Many conventional cleaning products release volatile organic compounds that can contribute to headaches, dizziness, and respiratory irritation tied to indoor air quality. Green products meaningfully reduce VOC exposure. For staff with asthma or chemical sensitivities, switching to green cleaning is often a genuine quality-of-life improvement.

Business Benefits

Brand alignment: if your business has sustainability commitments, your cleaning program should reflect them. Client perception: clients and employees generally look favorably on businesses that demonstrate environmental responsibility. Reduced liability: fewer harsh chemicals means reduced risk of chemical-related incidents.

The Cost Question

Green cleaning products have historically carried a price premium, but the gap has narrowed. When evaluating cleaning proposals, ask providers to specify which products are green-certified. Find out more about our product choices at the quote form.
