Red Flags to Watch Out for When Choosing an Office Cleaner

Most commercial cleaning companies present well in a sales conversation. The gaps show up later — in inconsistent service, mystery charges, or a different crew every other week. See our companion guide on how to choose an office cleaning company for the positive checklist; here's what to watch out for.

Red Flags Before You Sign

No written scope of work: if a company quotes you verbally but won't put the task list in writing, there's no accountability. Unusually low pricing: quotes significantly below market rate usually mean something is missing — underpaid staff, substandard products, or tasks that appear in the contract but rarely happen in practice. No proof of insurance: any legitimate cleaning company can produce a current certificate of insurance quickly. Hesitation is a sign they don't have it. Vague answers about who will clean your space: if they can't tell you whether staff are employees or subcontractors, proceed with caution. High-pressure sales tactics: artificial urgency is a manipulation tactic, not an indicator of a reputable business.

Red Flags During the Relationship

Constant staff turnover: a different crew every few weeks means no institutional knowledge of your space and inconsistent results. Ignored complaints: a company that takes days to respond to a service complaint, or disputes rather than resolves valid complaints, will not improve over time. Skipped tasks you've paid for: if items on your scope of work are regularly missed, document them and raise the issue formally. Surprise charges on invoices: additions that weren't agreed to in writing are a sign of billing practices that will escalate.
