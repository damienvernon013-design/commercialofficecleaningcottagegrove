Top 10 Areas Most Offices Miss During Cleaning

Even offices that look clean often harbor neglected spots that accumulate grime, bacteria, and allergens over time. If your cleaning service isn't addressing these areas, you're paying for a partial clean — and your employees and visitors are experiencing the difference, even if they can't name it.

1. Keyboard and Mouse Surfaces

Keyboards accumulate a surprising amount of bacteria, yet most cleaning crews skip them entirely without explicit instructions. Keyboards and mice should be disinfected at least weekly.

2. Light Switches and Outlet Plates

High-touch surfaces that almost never get wiped down in standard cleaning routines.

3. Elevator Buttons and Door Push Plates

Touched hundreds of times daily, these are among the highest-contact surfaces in any commercial building.

4. Tops of Filing Cabinets and Tall Shelving

Out of sight, out of mind — until a client reaches up for something and comes away with a dusty hand. High dusting requires extension tools and deliberate effort.

5. Refrigerator Handles and Microwave Buttons

Kitchen appliances see constant use but are often wiped only on the exterior surfaces.

6. Air Vents and Return Grilles

Dusty vents blow allergens and contaminants directly into breathing zones. These should be wiped monthly and deep-cleaned quarterly.

7. Chair Bases and Casters

Rolling chair wheels pick up debris from every floor surface and redistribute it.

8. Phone Handsets and Headsets

Used by mouth and ear multiple times per day, office phones are a significant germ vector that rarely appears on cleaning checklists.

9. Window Blinds and Tracks

Blinds accumulate dust rapidly and release it back into the air whenever they're opened or adjusted.

10. Under and Behind Furniture

Dust and debris accumulate under desks and behind furniture. Without periodic furniture-moving during deep cleans, these spots go unaddressed for months.
